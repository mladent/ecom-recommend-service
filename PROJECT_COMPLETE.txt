✨ ALGORITHMS IMPLEMENTED

Naive Bayes:
• Probabilistic classifier using Bayes' theorem
• Fast training and prediction
• Works well with high-dimensional data
• Output: Probability of bundle recommendation

SVM (Support Vector Machine):
• Discriminative classifier
• Finds optimal decision boundary
• Supports multiple kernels
• Handles non-linear relationships
• Output: Probability of bundle recommendation

Ensemble:
• Combines predictions from multiple models
• Averages probabilities
• More robust than single model
• Better generalization

Apriori (Bundle Discovery):
• Market basket analysis algorithm
• Finds frequently purchased product combinations
• Support and confidence filtering
• Generates itemsets of increasing size
• Output: List of product bundles
